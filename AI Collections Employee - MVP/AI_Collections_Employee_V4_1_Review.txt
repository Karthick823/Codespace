FINDINGS / REQUIRED FOLLOW-UP
HIGH PRIORITY
- Local username/password authentication is not enterprise SSO/MFA. Use an identity provider for a hosted SaaS deployment.
- The app is a large legacy Streamlit entry point with older workflow paths and multiple generations of services. UI hiding is not sufficient; each write/send/approval path must be tested at the service boundary.
- Tenant isolation is not demonstrated in the supplied files. Do not onboard multiple companies until tenant_id scoping and cross-tenant tests exist across every query and mutation.
- Audit rows are in the same SQLite database and are not tamper-evident against a database/file administrator. Production needs protected centralized/tamper-evident audit storage.
- Live email sending should remain disabled until recipient authorization, exact approved-version checks, delivery-unknown recovery, duplicate prevention, and real Graph permissions are validated in a controlled test tenant.

MEDIUM PRIORITY
- The archive has no requirements.txt/lock file. Reproducible dependency installation and vulnerability scanning are not currently possible from the supplied package alone.
- `sample_invoices.csv` is referenced by app.py but was not present in the archive. Confirm it is intentionally user-supplied or add a synthetic sample file.
- The archive includes a live-looking SQLite database and a backup. Do not share real customer data or production database files in source bundles; replace them with a synthetic/schema-only DB and add DB/backup files to .gitignore.
- Existing tests emit ResourceWarning messages for unclosed SQLite connections. Fix connection lifecycle/context management in tests/services.
- The `test_structured_output.py` test cannot import until the correct Google Gen AI dependency is installed in the environment. Add a pinned dependency manifest, then rerun the full suite.
- Review whether every sensitive export/report is role-appropriate and whether exported invoice/customer data is minimized.
- Confirm secrets are supplied only through environment/managed secret storage and never committed to source or logs.
